# App Review Response

Hello,

Thank you for reviewing QuoteCloser. We have revised the app to address Guidelines 5.1.1(i) and 5.1.2(i).

Mock AI mode is enabled by default and does not send client, quote, pricing, or voice-note data to any external AI service. We added in-app AI privacy notices on AI screens explaining this.

If a user enables Remote AI, the app now requires explicit permission before any AI generation action can send data. The consent screen explains:

- What data may be sent: business type, service type, client name and notes when included, quote pricing and scope details, selected tone/follow-up context, and voice transcripts only for Voice-to-Quote summaries.
- Who receives it: the QuoteCloser secure AI backend at `https://YOUR_BACKEND_URL.com/quotecloser-ai` and its AI generation provider.
- Why it is used: only to generate the requested proposal, upsell, objection reply, follow-up, or voice-note summary.

The privacy policy has also been updated to identify the data used by the app, how it is collected, how it is used, and the optional Remote AI sharing path.
