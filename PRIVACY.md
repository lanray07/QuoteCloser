# QuoteCloser Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 22, 2026

QuoteCloser is an iOS app for creating quotes, proposals, follow-up messages, objection replies, upsell suggestions, pipeline records, analytics, voice notes, and PDF proposal exports for service businesses.

This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

## Information You Add to the App

QuoteCloser may store information you enter, including:

- Business details
- Client or lead names
- Phone numbers
- Email addresses
- Addresses
- Service requests
- Quote pricing details
- Notes
- Follow-up messages
- Proposal content
- Uploaded job photos
- Voice note transcripts or summaries

This information is used to help you create and manage quotes, proposals, follow-ups, and sales-related content.

## Local Storage

QuoteCloser is designed to be offline-friendly. App data is stored locally on your device using Apple platform storage technologies.

The app does not require account registration or sign-in.

## AI Features

QuoteCloser includes AI-assisted features for proposals, upsells, objections, follow-ups, and voice note summaries.

Mock AI mode is enabled by default. In mock AI mode, generated content is produced locally from built-in sample logic and client, quote, pricing, photo, and voice-note data is not sent to an external AI service.

The app also includes a placeholder for a secure backend AI endpoint. Remote AI is optional and requires in-app permission before an AI generation action sends personal or business data. If remote AI is configured, enabled, and permitted by the user, the app may send the following data to the QuoteCloser secure AI backend at `https://YOUR_BACKEND_URL.com/quotecloser-ai` and that backend's AI generation provider:

- Business type and service type
- Client name and client notes when included in the selected quote
- Quote pricing details, scope notes, selected tone, and follow-up context
- Voice transcripts only when using Voice-to-Quote summarising

This data is used only to generate the proposal, upsell, objection reply, follow-up, or voice-note summary requested by the user and return it to the app. API keys should never be stored inside the app.

## Photos, Camera, Microphone, and Speech Recognition

QuoteCloser may request permission to use:

- Photos, so you can attach job-site images to quotes
- Camera, so you can capture new job-site images
- Microphone, so you can record voice notes
- Speech recognition, so voice notes can be converted into text

These permissions are optional and are used only for the features you choose to use.

## Purchases and Subscriptions

QuoteCloser includes StoreKit subscription scaffolding. Purchases and subscription management are handled by Apple. QuoteCloser does not receive or store your full payment card details.

## PDFs and Sharing

QuoteCloser can generate PDF proposal files and open the native iOS share sheet. When you choose to share a PDF, the destination app or service you select may process that file according to its own privacy policy.

## Analytics

QuoteCloser includes local quote analytics based on the quote records you create in the app, such as acceptance rate, average quote value, pipeline value, and upsell value.

The app does not include third-party analytics tracking in the current version.

## Legal, Financial, and Pricing Disclaimer

AI-generated content should be reviewed before use. Pricing estimates are not guaranteed. QuoteCloser is not legal or financial advice. You are responsible for final contracts, pricing, and communications sent to clients.

## Children

QuoteCloser is intended for business use and is not directed to children.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as the app changes. Updates will be posted in this repository or on the official QuoteCloser support website.
